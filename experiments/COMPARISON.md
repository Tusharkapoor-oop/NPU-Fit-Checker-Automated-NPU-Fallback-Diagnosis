### Root Cause of CPU Fallbacks (INT8 model)

The 2 CPU-executed layers are `QuantizeLinear` and `DequantizeLinear` operators
inserted by Qualcomm's quantization workflow at the model boundary. They convert
FP32 host-side tensors to INT8 before entering the Hexagon NPU subgraph, and
back to FP32 at the output.

The Hexagon NPU does not accelerate these boundary adapter ops when they appear
in the QDQ wrapper form generated by the quantizer. This is a known, expected
pattern — not a defect in the model architecture.

**Fix**: Configure model compilation with native INT8 input/output tensor types,
or move the quantization/dequantization step into the host preprocessing pipeline
outside the ONNX graph boundary.
